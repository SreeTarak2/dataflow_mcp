Use EVENT DATA's registration.status, eventDates, and audienceScope to frame
the event honestly:

  DATA does not state.
- registration.status = "closed" or "waitlist": do not say "register now".

  men can attend.
- audienceScope = "all": Frame as gender-inclusive. Avoid gendered language
  unless the event specifically targets a gender.
- audienceScope = null: Do not make gender assumptions. Use neutral framing.
